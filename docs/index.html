Why these numbers are different

Sourced assigns an opportunity to its original marketing acquisition channel. Influenced includes any sales-accepted opportunity with a recorded marketing touch, even one originally sourced by Sales. Deals can appear in multiple channel-specific influenced views. Never add influenced results across channels or combine them with sourced pipeline.

Qualified pipeline created includes accepted opportunities even if later won or lost, whereas open pipeline only includes currently open deals. Closed-won bookings are not automatically recognized revenue. Cost per accepted opportunity is not CAC or marketing ROI.
